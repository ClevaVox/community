# Clevaway Stewardship Policy

**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Proposed

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the relationship between Clevaway and ClevaVox.

Clevaway is the founding and supporting organisation of ClevaVox, a community-driven open-source initiative dedicated to linguistic inclusion and accessible artificial intelligence.

## 2. Responsibilities of Clevaway

Clevaway may provide:

- Initial leadership and administrative support.
- Technical infrastructure and computing resources.
- Engineering and research support.
- Community development and outreach.
- Institutional partnerships.
- Fundraising and sponsorship coordination.
- Operational and legal support, where authorized.
- Long-term organisational stewardship.

## 3. Community governance

ClevaVox shall maintain a publicly documented governance framework.

Clevaway shall respect the delegated authority of the Steering Committee, maintainers and working groups established under that framework.

Governance responsibilities shall progressively expand beyond the founding members as the community grows.

## 4. Open-source commitment

ClevaVox shall develop and distribute its software, models, datasets and documentation under appropriate publicly available licenses.

Clevaway shall not claim exclusive ownership of community contributions solely because it founded or supports the initiative.

Contributors shall retain applicable rights to their contributions.

## 5. Commercial activities

Clevaway may use publicly released ClevaVox technologies for commercial or noncommercial purposes under the same applicable licensing terms as other users.

Commercial products and services operated by Clevaway shall remain distinguishable from the community initiative.

Any preferential access to community resources, exclusive arrangements or related-party transactions must be disclosed and appropriately approved.

## 6. Financial separation

Financial resources received specifically for ClevaVox shall be separately accounted for and administered according to their declared purposes.

Clevaway's commercial revenue and operational expenses shall remain distinguishable from ClevaVox funding and expenditure.

Financial and material support provided by Clevaway may be recorded as founding support, sponsorship or in-kind contributions.

## 7. Transparency and accountability

Clevaway shall support the publication of:

- Project governance policies.
- Major strategic decisions.
- Approved sponsorship arrangements.
- Periodic financial reports.
- Disclosed conflicts of interest.
- Relevant changes to the stewardship relationship.

Confidential personal, contractual and security information shall be appropriately protected.

## 8. Project continuity

Essential project assets shall be administered in a manner that supports the long-term continuity of ClevaVox.

Any transition to an alternative stewardship arrangement, fiscal host or independent legal entity shall be conducted through an approved governance process and in accordance with applicable obligations.

## 9. Review and amendments

This policy shall be reviewed periodically.

Material changes shall follow the established governance approval process and be publicly documented.
