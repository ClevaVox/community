# Turaco Research — Clevaway Stewardship Policy

**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Proposed  
**Founding and supporting organisation:** Clevaway  
**Effective date:** To be determined

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the relationship between Clevaway and Turaco Research, including their respective responsibilities, governance arrangements, financial obligations, intellectual property principles and commitments to institutional continuity.

Its purpose is to ensure that the support provided by Clevaway strengthens the development of Turaco Research while preserving the initiative's open-source mission, community participation and transparent governance.

## 2. Institutional identity

Turaco Research is a community-driven open-source AI research initiative founded and supported by Clevaway.

The initiative is dedicated to linguistic inclusion, openly accessible artificial intelligence and computational efficiency, with particular emphasis on lightweight, edge-first and offline-capable technologies for underrepresented languages.

Turaco Research shall maintain a distinct public identity, research mission, governance framework and community structure.

Clevaway shall be publicly recognized as the founding and supporting organisation of Turaco Research.

Such recognition does not, by itself, establish exclusive ownership of community contributions or unrestricted authority over community governance.

## 3. Responsibilities of Clevaway

Clevaway may support Turaco Research through:

- Administrative and operational assistance.
- Technical infrastructure and hosting resources.
- Computing resources for research and model development.
- Software engineering and technical expertise.
- Research and institutional partnerships.
- Community development and outreach.
- Fundraising and sponsorship coordination.
- Legal and contractual support, where authorized.
- Financial administration, where appropriate.
- Public communications and institutional representation.
- Long-term organisational stewardship.

The nature and extent of support shall depend on available resources, approved arrangements and applicable legal obligations.

Clevaway is not required to provide unlimited financial, technical or operational resources.

Material commitments shall be agreed upon and appropriately documented.

## 4. Responsibilities of Turaco Research

Turaco Research shall:

- Maintain a clearly defined research mission.
- Operate according to its approved founding charter and governance framework.
- Promote open-source research and development.
- Establish transparent contribution and leadership procedures.
- Support meaningful participation by language communities.
- Apply responsible data collection and AI development practices.
- Maintain appropriate technical and research standards.
- Administer allocated resources responsibly.
- Publish relevant operational and financial information.
- Develop sustainable community leadership.

Turaco Research shall acknowledge the support provided by Clevaway through appropriate public documentation.

## 5. Governance and delegated authority

During the founding stage, Turaco Research shall operate under the leadership of its two co-founders in accordance with the Founders' Agreement.

Governance and operational responsibilities shall progressively be delegated to the Steering Committee, maintainers, working group leads and other qualified community representatives.

Clevaway shall respect the authority established under the approved governance framework.

Administrative control over repositories, infrastructure, domains or other project assets shall not be exercised to circumvent approved governance procedures.

Clevaway may raise concerns regarding legal obligations, financial risk, security, resource availability or material reputational risks.

Such concerns shall be communicated through the appropriate governance process.

Where immediate action is legally required or necessary to prevent material harm, temporary protective measures may be taken, provided they are proportionate, documented and subject to subsequent review.

## 6. Open-source commitment

Turaco Research shall develop and distribute its software, AI models, datasets, research and documentation under appropriate publicly documented licenses.

Open licensing shall be prioritized wherever legally and ethically possible.

Clevaway shall not require exclusive ownership of community contributions merely because it provides institutional, financial or technical support.

Publicly released resources shall remain governed by their applicable licenses.

Changes in stewardship, leadership or commercial strategy shall not invalidate rights already granted under existing licenses.

Resources that cannot be distributed under open licenses because of legal, ethical, privacy or third-party restrictions shall be clearly identified.

## 7. Intellectual property

Intellectual property rights shall be governed by applicable law, contribution agreements, employment obligations and approved project licenses.

Contributors shall retain applicable rights to their original contributions unless a separate written agreement establishes otherwise.

Clevaway shall retain applicable rights to its pre-existing intellectual property and proprietary resources.

Where Clevaway contributes software, models, datasets or other resources to Turaco Research, the applicable licensing terms shall be documented.

Institutional support shall not automatically grant Clevaway exclusive rights to resources independently developed by community contributors.

Any proposed copyright assignment, exclusive licensing arrangement or transfer of community intellectual property shall require appropriate authorization and compliance with the established governance framework.

## 8. Commercial activities

Clevaway may use publicly released Turaco Research technologies for commercial and noncommercial purposes under the same applicable licensing terms as other users.

Such activities may include:

- Hosted AI inference services.
- Enterprise language technology integrations.
- Custom model deployment and optimization.
- Research and engineering services.
- Commercial applications incorporating publicly available models.
- Technical consulting and support.

Clevaway's commercial activities shall remain distinguishable from the community activities of Turaco Research.

The use of Turaco Research technologies in commercial products shall not automatically imply official endorsement by the initiative.

Clevaway shall not receive preferential access to restricted community resources without an appropriately approved and documented arrangement.

Community resources shall not be diverted to proprietary commercial activities without authorization and compliance with applicable funding conditions.

Commercial adoption by Clevaway or other organisations shall be considered compatible with the initiative's mission when it respects the applicable licenses and community policies.

## 9. Financial separation

Financial resources received specifically for Turaco Research shall be separately accounted for and administered according to their declared purposes.

These resources may include:

- Research grants.
- Public donations.
- Corporate sponsorships.
- Institutional funding.
- Computing credits.
- Equipment and other material contributions.

Turaco Research funding shall remain distinguishable from Clevaway's unrestricted commercial revenue.

Clevaway may provide financial or material support to Turaco Research.

Such support may be recorded as founding support, sponsorship, grants, in-kind contributions or another appropriately documented arrangement.

Project funds shall not be used to subsidize unrelated commercial activities.

The administration of funds shall comply with applicable law, donor restrictions, contractual obligations and the approved financial governance policy.

Where appropriate, Turaco Research may adopt a dedicated fiscal hosting arrangement or other suitable financial administration structure.

## 10. Related-party transactions

Transactions between Turaco Research and Clevaway shall be conducted transparently and in accordance with the initiative's approved policies.

These transactions may include:

- Infrastructure and hosting services.
- Engineering or consulting services.
- Administrative support.
- Equipment purchases or rentals.
- Contributor compensation.
- Shared operational expenses.

Material related-party transactions shall include documentation of:

- The nature and purpose of the transaction.
- The resources or services involved.
- The applicable financial terms.
- The interests of the parties involved.
- The authorization and approval process.

Individuals with a material conflict of interest shall not independently approve the affected transaction.

Where independent approval is required but no suitable independent authority exists, the transaction shall not proceed until an appropriate review mechanism is established.

## 11. Funding and institutional partnerships

Clevaway may assist Turaco Research in identifying and securing funding, research partnerships, institutional collaborations and material support.

Funding arrangements shall remain consistent with the initiative's mission and applicable governance policies.

Financial or material support shall not automatically grant sponsors:

- Ownership of community-developed intellectual property.
- Governance or voting rights.
- Exclusive control over research outcomes.
- Preferential access to restricted datasets.
- Authority over community leadership appointments.

The legal recipient and administrator of project funding shall be clearly identified.

Only an appropriately authorized legal person or organisation may enter into binding contractual commitments on behalf of the initiative.

## 12. Transparency and public accountability

Clevaway shall support the publication of relevant information concerning its stewardship of Turaco Research.

Such information may include:

- Approved governance policies.
- Material stewardship decisions.
- Financial and material contributions.
- Approved sponsorship arrangements.
- Related-party transactions.
- Material conflicts of interest.
- Periodic financial and operational reports.
- Significant changes to institutional responsibilities.

Information shall be published in accordance with applicable privacy, security, legal and contractual requirements.

Confidential information shall not be disclosed unnecessarily.

Confidentiality shall not be used to conceal material misconduct, unauthorized expenditure or improper interference with community governance.

## 13. Project assets and infrastructure

Assets supporting Turaco Research may include:

- GitHub organisations and repositories.
- Model and dataset hosting accounts.
- Domains and websites.
- Cloud infrastructure.
- Computing equipment.
- Research records.
- Financial administration accounts.
- Brand and trademark assets.
- Community communication platforms.

The legal ownership, administrative responsibility and authorized access arrangements for essential project assets shall be documented.

Assets provided by Clevaway shall remain subject to their applicable ownership and usage arrangements.

Administrative access shall be allocated according to operational responsibility and security requirements.

Appropriate backup, recovery and succession procedures shall be established to minimize dependence on individual founders or administrators.

Where assets cannot legally or contractually be transferred, suitable alternatives and continuity arrangements shall be considered.

## 14. Branding and public representation

Turaco Research shall maintain a distinct public identity consistent with its research mission and community-oriented nature.

Clevaway shall receive appropriate recognition as the founding and supporting organisation.

The names, logos and other identifying marks of Clevaway and Turaco Research shall be governed by their applicable ownership arrangements and trademark policies.

Neither community members nor external organisations may represent themselves as authorized institutional representatives without appropriate permission.

Commercial use of publicly available software or models does not automatically authorize the use of the Turaco Research or Clevaway brand.

## 15. Institutional continuity and transition

Clevaway and the founders shall support the long-term continuity of Turaco Research.

The initiative may consider alternative institutional arrangements where necessary for its sustainability, including:

- An independent fiscal host.
- A nonprofit organisation.
- A research foundation.
- A consortium of supporting institutions.
- Another suitable stewardship organisation.

Any material transition shall follow the established governance process and applicable legal and contractual obligations.

The transition process shall address:

- Project assets and administrative access.
- Existing financial commitments.
- Restricted grants and sponsorship obligations.
- Intellectual property and licensing arrangements.
- Research records and datasets.
- Community communications.
- Operational continuity.

A change in Clevaway's ownership, commercial strategy or legal status should not unnecessarily disrupt the continuation of Turaco Research.

The applicable parties shall cooperate in establishing lawful and practical continuity arrangements.

## 16. Disagreements and conflicts

Disagreements concerning the stewardship relationship shall initially be addressed through constructive discussion between Clevaway representatives and the responsible Turaco Research governance authority.

Where a material conflict cannot be resolved internally, independent mediation or review may be sought.

The process shall respect applicable legal obligations, the Founders' Agreement and approved community governance procedures.

Material decisions and their outcomes shall be documented, subject to appropriate confidentiality requirements.

## 17. Policy review and amendments

This policy shall be reviewed at least annually and whenever a significant change occurs in the institutional relationship between Clevaway and Turaco Research.

Material amendments shall follow the approved governance process.

During the founding stage, amendments require the approval of both founders and the appropriate authorized representation of Clevaway.

As community governance matures, amendments shall additionally follow the established community consultation and approval procedures.

Amendments shall be publicly documented and version-controlled.

## 18. Adoption

This policy shall become effective upon approval through the applicable founding and institutional authorization procedures.

Turaco Research and Clevaway acknowledge their shared commitment to linguistic inclusion, open-source research, responsible innovation, computational accessibility and long-term community sustainability.
