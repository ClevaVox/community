# Turaco Research — Community

**Every language. Every device. Everyone.**

Welcome to the central community and governance repository of Turaco Research.

Turaco Research is a community-driven, open-source AI research initiative dedicated to advancing linguistic inclusion and computational accessibility through lightweight, edge-first artificial intelligence for underrepresented languages.

Founded and supported by Clevaway, we bring together researchers, engineers, linguists, native speakers, institutions and volunteers to collaboratively develop accessible language technologies.

## 1. About This Repository

This repository serves as the central source of truth for the governance, organisation and community operations of Turaco Research.

It provides access to:

- Founding principles and institutional policies.
- Governance and decision-making procedures.
- Leadership and maintainer responsibilities.
- Working group mandates.
- Community participation opportunities.
- Research and operational proposals.
- Project planning and progress records.
- Shared templates and operational resources.
- Transparency and accountability documentation.

Research implementations, model training pipelines, datasets and supporting software may be maintained in dedicated repositories within the Turaco Research organisation.

## 2. Our Mission

Our mission is to collaboratively research, develop, maintain and openly distribute efficient artificial intelligence models, datasets and technologies for underrepresented languages, dialects and linguistic varieties.

Our work is guided by three founding pillars:

- **Linguistic Inclusion:** Every language deserves meaningful digital representation.
- **Open Source:** Research, knowledge and technologies should be accessible and collaborative.
- **Edge-First AI:** Artificial intelligence should be practical on affordable devices, with minimal dependence on cloud infrastructure.

Read our [Founding Charter](CHARTER.md) to learn more about our vision, mission and founding commitments.

## 3. Getting Started

Participation in Turaco Research is open to individuals and organisations that support our mission.

You do not need to have a technical background to contribute.

If you are new to the community:

1. Read our [Founding Charter](CHARTER.md).
2. Explore our [Working Groups](working-groups/README.md).
3. Introduce yourself through [Community Discussions](https://github.com/turaco-research/community/discussions).
4. Identify an area where your skills, interests or language knowledge can contribute.
5. Connect with the relevant working group or maintainer.
6. Explore available tasks, research proposals and contribution opportunities.

If you are unfamiliar with GitHub or unsure where to begin, you are welcome to request assistance through our community discussions.

Formal contribution, licensing and data submission procedures will be published as they are established.

## 4. Ways to Contribute

Turaco Research recognizes meaningful contributions across technical, scientific, linguistic and operational disciplines.

| Area | Contribution opportunities |
|---|---|
| AI Research | Model architectures, training, fine-tuning, experimentation and evaluation |
| Software Engineering | Development tools, infrastructure, applications and integrations |
| Edge AI | Model compression, quantization, efficient inference and device benchmarking |
| Linguistics | Language documentation, linguistic standards and cultural validation |
| Native Language Knowledge | Authentic language usage, pronunciation and contextual validation |
| Data Contributions | Authorized recordings, translations, transcriptions and annotations |
| Quality Assurance | Model testing, dataset validation and performance evaluation |
| Documentation | Tutorials, research documentation and contributor guides |
| Community Development | Outreach, onboarding, education and contributor support |
| Institutional Collaboration | Research partnerships, expertise and educational resources |
| Financial and Material Support | Grants, sponsorships, computing resources and equipment |

All meaningful forms of participation are valued.

Contributor recognition shall not be limited to GitHub activity, software commits or academic qualifications.

Do not upload personal recordings, copyrighted materials, confidential information or datasets containing personal data unless the applicable contribution and data governance requirements have been satisfied.

## 5. Working Groups

Turaco Research organizes its activities through collaborative working groups.

| Working Group | Focus | Mandate |
|---|---|---|
| AI Research | Model development, scientific research and evaluation | [View mandate](working-groups/ai-research.md) |
| Data and Linguistics | Dataset development, linguistic validation and responsible data practices | [View mandate](working-groups/data-and-linguistics.md) |

| Community | Contributor support, outreach, education and participation | [View mandate](working-groups/community.md) |

## 6. Language Projects

Turaco Research is global in scope and welcomes proposals concerning underrepresented languages, dialects and linguistic varieties.

Language-specific projects may bring together native speakers, linguists, AI researchers, engineers, data contributors and other community members.

Language communities shall be treated as active participants in research and development rather than merely as sources of training data.

Individuals interested in proposing a new language project should review the [Language-specific Working Group Framework](working-groups/languages/README.md) and initiate a community discussion.

Approved language projects and their respective leadership arrangements will be documented as they are established.

## 7. Governance

Turaco Research operates under a publicly documented governance framework designed to support transparent leadership, meaningful community participation and progressive delegation of responsibilities.

The initiative is initially stewarded by its two founders, with governance responsibilities progressively expanding to qualified community members.

| [Maintainer Registry](MAINTAINERS.md) | Identifies individuals holding formal governance and operational responsibilities |

## 8. Leadership and Responsibilities

Formal governance and operational responsibilities are documented in the [Maintainer Registry](MAINTAINERS.md).

Leadership responsibilities are assigned according to demonstrated competence, commitment, integrity, reliability and the needs of the initiative.

Community members may progressively assume greater responsibilities through the appointment and progression procedures established in the Governance Framework.

Meaningful technical and nontechnical contributions shall receive appropriate consideration.

Membership in a GitHub team does not independently establish formal governance authority or unrestricted access to project resources.

## 9. Discussions, Issues and Proposals

Turaco Research encourages open, constructive and documented collaboration.

### Community Discussions

Use [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/turaco-research/community/discussions) for:

- Introducing yourself.
- Asking questions.
- Exploring research ideas.
- Proposing language projects.
- Seeking contribution opportunities.
- Providing community feedback.
- Discussing governance and institutional matters.
- Requesting guidance.

### GitHub Issues

Use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/turaco-research/community/issues) for actionable work items, documentation improvements and identifiable community or operational problems.

Technical issues relating to a specific model, dataset or software project should ordinarily be raised in the relevant repository.

### Pull Requests

Pull requests shall be used to propose changes to repository documentation, approved policies, templates and other version-controlled resources.

Changes shall follow the applicable review and approval procedures.

### Requests for Comments

Material research, governance, architectural and institutional proposals shall follow the Request for Comments (RFC) process established in the Governance Framework.

The process enables community consultation, documented review and transparent resolution of significant proposals.

Public discussion does not independently constitute formal authorization.

## 12. Responsible Research and Data Practices

Turaco Research is committed to responsible data collection, scientific integrity, linguistic authenticity and computational accessibility.

Research and contribution activities shall respect:

- Informed consent.
- Personal privacy.
- Intellectual property rights.
- Cultural and linguistic context.
- Applicable data protection requirements.
- Research reproducibility.
- Transparent performance evaluation.
- Responsible model and dataset publication.

Model efficiency shall be evaluated alongside linguistic quality, accuracy, reliability and practical usefulness.

Detailed data governance, model release and technical standards will be published through dedicated policies.

Until the relevant procedures are established, participation in community discussions does not constitute authorization to submit, process or publish sensitive data.

## 13. Funding, Support and Partnerships

Turaco Research welcomes mission-aligned support from individuals, organisations and institutions.

Support may include:

- Research grants.
- Financial sponsorships.
- Computing resources and cloud credits.
- Hardware and testing equipment.
- Responsibly licensed linguistic resources.
- Scientific and technical expertise.
- Academic collaboration.
- Educational and community outreach.

Financial and material support shall be administered according to approved governance policies and applicable funding obligations.

Sponsorship or institutional support does not automatically confer governance authority or exclusive ownership of community-developed resources.

Formal donation channels, funding procedures and financial reporting arrangements will be published once established.

Potential supporters may initiate contact through the community discussions without disclosing sensitive financial or contractual information publicly.

## 14. Community Conduct

Turaco Research seeks to maintain a respectful, inclusive and constructive environment.

Community participants are expected to:

- Treat others with respect.
- Recognize linguistic and cultural diversity.
- Engage in constructive and good-faith discussion.
- Respect contributor privacy.
- Acknowledge the work of others.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination and abusive conduct.
- Maintain scientific and professional integrity.
- Respect approved governance and contribution procedures.

A formal Code of Conduct and enforcement procedure will be published as part of the contribution framework.

Sensitive complaints should not be posted publicly. Until a dedicated reporting channel is established, contributors should seek an appropriate private contact through the responsible governance representatives.

## 15. Transparency and Accountability

Turaco Research is committed to transparent governance, scientific research, resource management and institutional accountability.

Public records may include:

- Approved governance policies.
- Leadership appointments.
- Working group progress reports.
- Research proposals and decisions.
- Strategic roadmaps.
- Research outcomes.
- Model and dataset evaluations.
- Financial and sponsorship reports.
- Significant institutional decisions.

Records shall be maintained with appropriate consideration for privacy, security, legal requirements and legitimate confidentiality obligations.

## 16. Licensing

Turaco Research aims to release its software, models, datasets and documentation under appropriate open licenses wherever legally and ethically possible.

Different types of resources may be governed by different licenses.

Contributors and users should consult the license associated with each repository, model, dataset or published resource.

The license applicable to this repository is identified in its LICENSE file once formally adopted.

A comprehensive licensing and contribution policy will establish the applicable requirements for future contributions.

## 17. Current Status

Turaco Research is currently in its founding and institutional development stage.

Our immediate priorities are:

- Completing the foundational governance framework.
- Establishing licensing and contribution procedures.
- Developing responsible data governance policies.
- Establishing financial and sponsorship procedures.
- Defining technical research and edge AI standards.
- Selecting and preparing initial language projects.
- Developing sustainable community participation mechanisms.

We welcome early participants interested in helping shape the future of the initiative.

No research model, dataset, partnership or funding arrangement should be considered officially established unless it has been appropriately documented and approved.
