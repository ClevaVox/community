## 1. Purpose

This document serves as the authoritative public registry of individuals holding formal leadership, governance, maintenance and operational responsibilities within Turaco Research.

The registry establishes visibility over:

- Active leadership appointments.
- Delegated responsibilities.
- Areas of accountability.
- Working group leadership.
- Project and repository maintenance.
- Appointment status.
- Leadership succession and continuity.

Appointments and responsibilities documented in this registry shall comply with the [Governance Framework](GOVERNANCE.md) and [Founding Charter](CHARTER.md).

## 9. Appointment statuses

The following statuses shall be used to describe leadership and maintainer appointments.

- **Active:** Confirmed appointment with current delegated responsibilities.
- **Provisional:** Appointment undergoing an initial assessment period.
- **On leave:** Temporarily unavailable under an agreed arrangement.
- **Vacant:** Position has not been filled.
- **Suspended:** Delegated authority has been temporarily suspended through an applicable procedure.
- **Former:** Individual previously held the role but no longer exercises its authority.

Suspension, removal and reassignment shall follow the applicable Governance Framework.

Sensitive personal or disciplinary information shall not be unnecessarily disclosed in this registry.

## 10. Appointment records

Formal appointments shall document, where applicable:

- Public contributor identity.
- Assigned role.
- Relevant working group or project.
- Scope of responsibility.
- Appointment date.
- Appointment status.
- Authorizing governance body or individuals.
- Reference to the approved appointment decision.
- Applicable review date.

The registry shall not contain passwords, private credentials, sensitive access information or unnecessary personal data.

## 11. Scope of delegated authority

The authority of each individual shall be limited to their approved appointment and applicable project policies.

Being listed as a Maintainer, Working Group Lead, Core Maintainer or Steering Committee member does not automatically grant:

- GitHub organisation ownership.
- Unrestricted repository permissions.
- Access to restricted datasets.
- Financial authorization.
- Contractual signing authority.
- Ownership of community-developed intellectual property.
- Authority to represent Turaco Research in every institutional matter.

Operational and technical access shall be managed separately according to security requirements and the principle of least privilege.

## 12. Multiple appointments

An individual may hold more than one role, particularly during the founding stage.

Each appointment shall have an identifiable scope of responsibility.

Holding multiple roles shall not grant additional voting rights within the same governance body.

Where a decision requires independent approval by multiple individuals, one person holding multiple roles shall not satisfy that requirement alone.

## 13. Registry maintenance

The Steering Committee shall oversee the accuracy and integrity of this registry.

Relevant governance authorities shall ensure that approved appointments, changes in responsibility and departures are reflected promptly.

Updates shall ordinarily be submitted through pull requests and reviewed according to the applicable governance procedures.

A registry update shall reference the corresponding approved appointment, reassignment or departure decision where appropriate.

A modification to this registry does not independently constitute authorization of an appointment.

## 14. Periodic review

Active leadership and maintainer appointments shall be reviewed at least every six months in accordance with the Governance Framework.

Reviews shall consider:

- Availability and participation.
- Assigned responsibilities.
- Contribution quality.
- Operational requirements.
- Security and access requirements.
- Leadership continuity.
- Opportunities for further delegation.

Historical founding recognition shall remain distinct from the review of active operational responsibilities.
