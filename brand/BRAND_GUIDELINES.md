# Turaco Research — Brand Guidelines

**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Proposed  
**Founded and supported by:** Clevaway

## 1. Purpose

These guidelines establish the visual identity, institutional positioning, communication principles and brand usage conventions of Turaco Research.

Their purpose is to maintain a consistent and recognizable identity across research publications, software projects, community communications, websites and institutional activities.

## 2. Brand identity

- **Official name:** Turaco Research
- **Short name:** Turaco
- **Tagline:** Every language. Every device. Everyone.
- **Institutional positioning:** Open-source AI research for underrepresented languages.
- **Founding organisation:** Clevaway

Turaco Research shall maintain a distinct public identity while acknowledging its founding and institutional relationship with Clevaway.

## 3. Brand foundations

The brand represents three foundational pillars:

- Linguistic inclusion.
- Open-source research and development.
- Edge-first artificial intelligence.

All official communications should remain consistent with the Founding Charter.

## 4. Brand personality

Turaco Research seeks to communicate an identity that is:

- Scientific.
- Inclusive.
- Innovative.
- Approachable.
- Trustworthy.
- Globally oriented.
- Computationally conscious.

The brand reflects its African origins while maintaining a global research identity.

## 5. Primary artwork

Turaco Research shall maintain a designated primary brand artwork representing its visual identity and creative direction.

The approved artwork may be used across:

- Organisation banners.
- Websites.
- Research presentations.
- Community announcements.
- Institutional communications.
- Educational materials.
- Approved promotional resources.

The original artwork shall be preserved within the designated brand assets directory.

The artwork shall serve as a creative reference for future visual materials but shall not automatically constitute the official organisation logo.

Its provenance, approval status and applicable usage terms shall be documented.

## 6. Colour palette

The proposed institutional colour palette consists of:

| Colour | HEX | Application |
|---|---|---|
| Turaco Forest | #174D3B | Primary brand colour |
| Research Charcoal | #172522 | Dark backgrounds and primary text |
| Natural Ivory | #F3F5F1 | Light backgrounds and publications |
| Canopy Green | #65A65D | Supporting visual elements |
| Crimson Feather | #BD3944 | Restrained brand accents |
| Muted Sage | #A6BBAA | Secondary backgrounds and supporting elements |

Visual materials should prioritize clarity, appropriate contrast and accessibility.

Crimson and other strong accent colours should be used selectively.

The palette shall be reviewed against the selected primary artwork before final adoption.

## 7. Typography

The proposed typography system consists of:

- **Space Grotesk:** Brand headings and institutional typography.
- **Noto Sans:** Body text, documentation and multilingual communication.
- **JetBrains Mono:** Technical content, code and benchmarks.
- **Noto Serif:** Academic publications where appropriate.

Additional typefaces from the Noto family may be used to support specific writing systems.

Typography shall prioritize readability, multilingual compatibility and accessibility.

## 8. Visual design principles

Official visual materials should emphasize:

- Clear visual hierarchy.
- Consistent spacing and alignment.
- Minimalist composition.
- Scientific clarity.
- Restrained use of colour.
- Appropriate contrast.
- Meaningful illustrations.
- Linguistic and cultural authenticity.

Generic technology imagery, misleading scientific illustrations and excessive decorative effects should be avoided.

## 9. Imagery

The visual identity may incorporate:

- Nature-inspired artwork.
- Scientific and linguistic visualizations.
- Authentic community photography.
- Research and engineering imagery.
- Computational and hardware illustrations.
- Abstract representations of speech and language.

Imagery should remain relevant to the initiative's mission.

Appropriate consent and usage rights shall be obtained for identifiable individuals and third-party materials.

AI-generated imagery shall not be presented as documentary evidence of actual people, events or research activities.

## 10. Communication principles

Official communications shall be:

- Clear and accessible.
- Scientifically responsible.
- Respectful of linguistic and cultural diversity.
- Transparent about research limitations.
- Accurate regarding institutional activities.
- Free from unsupported performance claims.
- Inclusive of technical and nontechnical audiences.

Research outcomes shall not be exaggerated for promotional purposes.

## 11. Brand architecture

Turaco Research shall serve as the primary institutional brand.

Research projects, models, datasets and technical tools may adopt consistent Turaco-based naming conventions where appropriate.

Project identities shall remain consistent with the institutional mission and approved visual guidelines.

## 12. Founding organisation recognition

Official institutional materials should acknowledge Clevaway using the following wording where appropriate:

**Founded and supported by Clevaway.**

Recognition of Clevaway shall not obscure the distinct identity or community-driven nature of Turaco Research.

## 13. Logo

The official Turaco Research logo shall be developed and approved separately.

Until an official logo is adopted, the organisation may use its approved artwork and institutional wordmark.

A future logo shall be compatible with the established visual identity.

## 14. Brand assets

Approved visual assets shall be maintained in a version-controlled location accessible to authorized contributors.

Assets may include:

- Primary artwork.
- Institutional wordmarks.
- Logos and symbols.
- Organisation banners.
- Research publication templates.
- Presentation templates.
- Community communication materials.
- Colour and typography references.

Assets shall be identified by their approval status and applicable usage terms.

## 15. Brand usage

Public availability of brand assets does not automatically authorize their use to imply institutional endorsement, official representation or affiliation.

Brand usage shall comply with applicable licenses, rights and approved institutional policies.

The licensing of software, models and datasets shall remain distinct from the usage conditions associated with institutional branding.

Detailed brand usage and trademark procedures shall be established separately.

## 16. Approval and maintenance

The Steering Committee shall oversee material changes to the institutional identity.

During founding governance, significant brand decisions shall require approval from both founders.

Routine visual materials consistent with approved guidelines may be produced by authorized working groups.

Brand guidelines and approved assets shall be maintained through version-controlled documentation.
