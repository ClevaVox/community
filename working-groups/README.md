# Turaco Research — Working Group Framework

**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Proposed  
**Effective date:** To be determined

## 1. Purpose

Working groups are operational units established to coordinate research, engineering, linguistic development, community participation and other activities supporting the mission of Turaco Research.

Working groups shall operate in accordance with the [Founding Charter](../CHARTER.md), [Governance Framework](../GOVERNANCE.md) and their respective approved mandates.

## 2. Established working groups

Turaco Research initially establishes the following working group mandates:

| Working group | Mandate |
|---|---|
| AI Research | [Mandate](ai-research.md) |
| Data and Linguistics | [Mandate](data-and-linguistics.md) |
| Edge AI | [Mandate](edge-ai.md) |
| Community | [Mandate](community.md) |
| Language-specific working groups | [Framework](languages/README.md) |

The establishment of a mandate does not independently confirm that a working group is operational or that its leadership has been appointed.

## 3. Membership

Participation in working groups shall be open to individuals supporting the initiative's mission and complying with applicable project policies.

Membership may include researchers, engineers, linguists, native speakers, students, volunteers, institutional representatives and other contributors.

Participation does not automatically confer formal leadership authority, repository permissions or access to restricted resources.

Formal appointments shall be documented in the [Maintainer Registry](../MAINTAINERS.md).

## 4. Leadership

Each active working group shall have an identified lead or an explicitly documented leadership arrangement.

Working Group Leads shall be appointed in accordance with the Governance Framework.

Their responsibilities include:

- Coordinating group activities.
- Establishing and maintaining a visible roadmap.
- Supporting and onboarding contributors.
- Facilitating collaboration and reviews.
- Monitoring expected deliverables.
- Reporting progress, risks and dependencies.
- Recommending additional maintainers.
- Maintaining operational continuity.

Working Group Leads shall exercise only the authority delegated through their appointments and approved mandates.

## 5. Operational autonomy

Working groups may make routine operational decisions within their approved scope.

Such decisions must comply with:

- Approved governance policies.
- Technical and research standards.
- Licensing requirements.
- Data consent and privacy obligations.
- Approved budgets and resource allocations.
- Applicable security requirements.
- Responsibilities delegated to other working groups.

Working groups shall not independently amend project-wide policies, authorize unapproved financial commitments or enter binding institutional agreements.

## 6. Cross-group collaboration

Working groups shall coordinate activities where responsibilities overlap or where one group's decisions materially affect another.

Model development shall incorporate relevant linguistic, computational efficiency, data governance and deployment requirements from the beginning of the research process.

Material disagreements shall be resolved through the appropriate technical or governance escalation procedures.

## 7. Planning and delivery

Active working groups shall maintain appropriate records of:

- Objectives and priorities.
- Research or operational activities.
- Assigned responsibilities.
- Expected deliverables.
- Dependencies.
- Resource requirements.
- Risks and limitations.
- Decisions and outcomes.

GitHub Issues, Projects, Discussions and public documentation should be used where appropriate.

## 8. Reporting

Active working groups should publish a progress summary at least monthly.

Reports should identify completed work, ongoing activities, research findings, resource requirements, material risks and upcoming priorities.

Sensitive personal, security, contractual or protected data shall not be included in public reports.

Inactive groups shall not be required to produce artificial progress reports but should have their operational status clearly documented.

## 9. Performance and accountability

Working group performance shall be evaluated according to its approved mandate.

Evaluation should consider quality, relevance, reliability, collaboration, research integrity and meaningful progress.

Performance indicators shall not encourage misleading reporting, excessive administrative activity or contribution quantity at the expense of quality.

## 10. Resource requests

Working groups may propose requests for computing resources, funding, infrastructure, equipment or external expertise.

Requests shall document their purpose, expected benefit, estimated requirements and relevant alternatives.

Resource approval shall follow the applicable financial and governance policies.

## 11. Lifecycle and review

A working group may have one of the following operational statuses:

- Proposed.
- Active.
- Paused.
- Inactive.
- Dissolved.

Working group mandates should be reviewed at least annually or when their responsibilities materially change.

Establishment, restructuring and dissolution shall follow the Governance Framework.

Research records, documentation and operational responsibilities shall be appropriately preserved when a group becomes inactive or is dissolved.
