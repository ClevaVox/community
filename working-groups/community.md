# Community Working Group — Mandate

**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Proposed  
**Governance authority:** Steering Committee

## 1. Purpose

The Community Working Group shall support the growth, accessibility, participation and long-term sustainability of the Turaco Research community.

Its objective is to enable individuals and institutions to contribute meaningfully to the initiative regardless of their technical expertise, geographical location or professional background.

## 2. Scope

Activities may include:

- Contributor recruitment.
- Contributor onboarding.
- Community engagement.
- Documentation and education.
- Language community outreach.
- Community events.
- Institutional outreach.
- Contributor recognition.
- Public communications.
- Community support.
- Feedback collection.

## 3. Contributor onboarding

The working group shall:

- Maintain accessible contribution guidance.
- Help new contributors identify relevant opportunities.
- Facilitate introductions to working groups.
- Support contributors unfamiliar with GitHub.
- Coordinate beginner-friendly tasks.
- Develop onboarding and educational resources.
- Identify barriers to participation.
- Facilitate mentorship where appropriate.

Participation opportunities shall accommodate both technical and nontechnical contributors.

## 4. Language community engagement

The working group shall collaborate with Data and Linguistics and language-specific working groups to:

- Engage native and fluent speakers.
- Support linguistic outreach.
- Recruit data contributors and validators.
- Coordinate community collection activities.
- Facilitate communication in appropriate languages.
- Encourage participation by underrepresented communities.

Data collection activities shall remain subject to applicable consent, privacy and data governance requirements.

## 5. Education and contributor development

The working group may coordinate:

- Research workshops.
- Technical tutorials.
- Contributor orientation sessions.
- Data annotation training.
- Language technology demonstrations.
- Educational documentation.
- Community learning activities.
- Student and academic participation initiatives.

Educational activities shall support the development of sustainable contributor expertise.

## 6. Community communications

The working group shall help maintain:

- Community announcements.
- Public documentation.
- Contributor guidance.
- Community discussion channels.
- Research progress summaries.
- Approved outreach materials.
- Public participation opportunities.

Communications shall accurately represent approved project positions and research outcomes.

Unapproved partnerships, funding arrangements or institutional commitments shall not be represented as official decisions.

## 7. Contributor recognition

The working group shall support recognition of meaningful contributions across all areas of the initiative.

Recognition shall not be limited to software commits.

Contributor recognition may include:

- Public contributor records.
- Release acknowledgments.
- Research acknowledgments.
- Community highlights.
- Recognition of linguistic and data contributions.
- Approved educational or professional references.

Research authorship shall follow applicable scientific and contributor recognition standards.

## 8. External support and institutional outreach

The working group may identify potential supporters, academic institutions, sponsors and collaborative organisations.

It may assist with outreach and proposal preparation.

Financial commitments, institutional agreements and sponsorship arrangements shall require the applicable governance, financial and legal approvals.

## 9. Community support and conduct

The working group shall facilitate constructive community participation and help direct contributors towards appropriate support channels.

Moderation and enforcement activities shall follow the Code of Conduct and applicable enforcement procedures.

Sensitive complaints shall be referred to appropriately authorized and impartial individuals.

The working group shall not independently impose disciplinary measures beyond its delegated authority.

## 10. Expected deliverables

Deliverables may include:

- Contributor onboarding guides.
- Community participation documentation.
- Educational resources.
- Outreach materials.
- Contributor recognition records.
- Community event reports.
- Public announcements.
- Community feedback summaries.
- Institutional collaboration proposals.
- Community progress reports.

## 11. Performance indicators

Progress may be evaluated through:

- Meaningful contributor participation.
- Contributor retention.
- Successful onboarding.
- Diversity of contribution types.
- Accessibility of participation resources.
- Community feedback.
- Language community engagement.
- Educational activities.
- Quality of contributor support.

Community size, social media visibility and GitHub stars alone shall not constitute evidence of meaningful community development.

## 12. Delegated authority

The working group may coordinate approved community activities, contributor support and routine communications within its mandate.

It shall not independently authorize financial commitments, establish binding partnerships, appoint governance leaders or alter approved project policies.

## 13. Reporting

The working group shall maintain visible participation objectives, community activity records and periodic progress summaries.

Public reports shall respect contributor privacy and applicable confidentiality requirements.
