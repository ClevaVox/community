# Data and Linguistics Working Group — Mandate

**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Proposed  
**Governance authority:** Steering Committee  
**Technical coordination:** Core Maintainers

## 1. Purpose

The Data and Linguistics Working Group shall coordinate the responsible collection, development, processing, validation and management of linguistic resources supporting Turaco Research.

Its objective is to establish high-quality, representative and appropriately authorized datasets for underrepresented languages.

## 2. Scope

The working group may support:

- Speech and audio datasets.
- Text corpora.
- Parallel translation datasets.
- Pronunciation dictionaries.
- Linguistic annotation resources.
- Language identification datasets.
- Evaluation benchmarks.
- Data collection tools.
- Dataset processing infrastructure.
- Linguistic research and documentation.

## 3. Data collection responsibilities

The working group shall:

- Identify linguistic data requirements.
- Establish appropriate collection methodologies.
- Review potential external data sources.
- Coordinate recording, transcription and translation activities.
- Document dataset provenance.
- Verify applicable usage and redistribution rights.
- Establish contributor consent requirements.
- Coordinate data collection with language communities.
- Identify potential gaps in linguistic and demographic coverage.

## 4. Linguistic responsibilities

The working group shall collaborate with relevant language-specific working groups to:

- Establish transcription and annotation guidelines.
- Document linguistic variations.
- Support orthographic and pronunciation conventions.
- Validate translations and transcriptions.
- Preserve relevant regional and cultural context.
- Identify linguistic errors and harmful biases.
- Establish language-specific evaluation methodologies.
- Facilitate native speaker participation.

The working group shall not impose a single linguistic standard where legitimate language variations require representation.

## 5. Data engineering responsibilities

The working group shall coordinate:

- Data ingestion and processing.
- Cleaning and normalization.
- Format standardization.
- Quality validation.
- Deduplication.
- Dataset versioning.
- Metadata management.
- Data lineage and provenance tracking.
- Training, validation and test split preparation.
- Dataset documentation.

Evaluation datasets shall be appropriately separated from training data to support reliable model evaluation.

## 6. Consent and data governance

Data collection and publication shall comply with applicable law and approved data governance policies.

The working group shall consider:

- Informed consent.
- Intellectual property rights.
- Privacy and personal data protection.
- Cultural sensitivity.
- Community interests.
- Data licensing.
- Authorized AI training purposes.
- Authorized redistribution.
- Security and access restrictions.

Permission to use speech data for recognition shall not automatically constitute permission to use it for voice synthesis or voice replication.

Restricted or unapproved datasets shall not be published merely because they are available to technical contributors.

## 7. Expected deliverables

Deliverables may include:

- Data collection guidelines.
- Consent requirements.
- Annotation standards.
- Validated datasets.
- Dataset cards.
- Data processing pipelines.
- Data quality reports.
- Linguistic reference documentation.
- Language coverage assessments.
- Evaluation datasets.
- Dataset provenance records.

Sensitive consent records shall be maintained through appropriate restricted systems.

## 8. Performance indicators

Progress may be evaluated through:

- Dataset quality and validation outcomes.
- Linguistic and regional coverage.
- Completeness of provenance records.
- Licensing and consent verification.
- Annotation consistency.
- Representation of authentic language usage.
- Data processing reproducibility.
- Suitability of datasets for approved research objectives.

Dataset volume alone shall not constitute evidence of quality or research success.

## 9. Delegated authority

The working group may coordinate routine data collection, processing and linguistic validation activities within approved policies.

It may withhold its approval of a proposed dataset or model release where credible concerns exist regarding consent, privacy, licensing, linguistic representation or data quality.

Such concerns shall be documented and resolved through the applicable release and governance procedures.

The working group shall not independently override valid consent restrictions or legal obligations.

## 10. Collaboration

The working group shall collaborate with:

- AI Research on training and evaluation data requirements.
- Edge AI on datasets suitable for efficient model development.
- Community on contributor recruitment and collection activities.
- Language-specific groups on linguistic standards and validation.
- Core Maintainers on technical infrastructure and release standards.

## 11. Reporting

The working group shall maintain visible progress records, dataset documentation and appropriate data quality reports.

Public reporting shall exclude protected personal information and confidential consent records.
