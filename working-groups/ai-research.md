# AI Research Working Group — Mandate

**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Proposed  
**Governance authority:** Steering Committee  
**Technical coordination:** Core Maintainers

## 1. Purpose

The AI Research Working Group shall coordinate artificial intelligence research and model development supporting linguistic inclusion and computational accessibility.

Its objective is to develop reliable, reproducible and efficient language technologies for underrepresented languages.

## 2. Research scope

Research activities may include:

- Automatic speech recognition.
- Text-to-speech and speech synthesis.
- Machine translation.
- Language identification.
- Natural language understanding and generation.
- Multilingual and cross-lingual learning.
- Transfer learning.
- Model architecture research.
- Efficient training methodologies.
- Synthetic data research.
- Knowledge distillation.
- Model evaluation and benchmarking.

Research priorities shall remain consistent with the Founding Charter and approved project roadmap.

## 3. Responsibilities

The working group shall:

- Identify and document relevant research problems.
- Review existing scientific literature and available models.
- Establish reproducible baseline experiments.
- Evaluate suitable model architectures.
- Develop training and fine-tuning pipelines.
- Document experiment configurations and outcomes.
- Compare proposed approaches against appropriate baselines.
- Investigate methods suitable for low-resource languages.
- Collaborate with Edge AI on efficient architecture design.
- Collaborate with Data and Linguistics on dataset requirements.
- Coordinate technical evaluation of trained models.
- Document model limitations and research findings.
- Support the preparation of official model releases.
- Encourage independent verification and research collaboration.

## 4. Edge-first research requirements

Computational accessibility shall be considered during research planning and architecture selection.

Research proposals should identify, where applicable:

- Intended deployment environments.
- Target hardware categories.
- Expected model size.
- Memory and computational requirements.
- Latency objectives.
- Accuracy and quality requirements.
- Potential optimization techniques.

The working group may use large models and high-performance computing during experimentation and training.

Research intended for practical deployment should identify a credible pathway towards efficient local inference.

## 5. Research integrity

Research activities shall:

- Use appropriately authorized data and model resources.
- Maintain traceable experiment records.
- Avoid contamination between training and evaluation datasets.
- Document evaluation methodologies.
- Report material limitations and unsuccessful outcomes.
- Avoid unsupported performance claims.
- Respect applicable attribution and authorship standards.

## 6. Expected deliverables

Depending on approved research priorities, deliverables may include:

- Research proposals.
- Literature reviews.
- Baseline models.
- Reproducible training scripts.
- Experiment configurations.
- Evaluation reports.
- Fine-tuned models.
- Research documentation.
- Model cards.
- Research papers and technical reports.
- Model improvement recommendations.

## 7. Performance indicators

The working group's progress may be evaluated through:

- Reproducibility of experiments.
- Improvement against established baselines.
- Linguistic quality across supported language varieties.
- Computational efficiency improvements.
- Quality and completeness of research documentation.
- Successful cross-group collaboration.
- Practical usefulness of research outcomes.

No universal accuracy or model size target shall apply across all research tasks.

## 8. Delegated authority

The working group may propose and conduct approved research activities and make routine research decisions within its mandate.

It shall not independently authorize the publication of restricted datasets, bypass mandatory model release reviews or modify project-wide licensing and governance policies.

## 9. Collaboration

The working group shall collaborate with:

- Data and Linguistics on data quality, linguistic coverage and evaluation.
- Edge AI on model architecture, optimization and deployment.
- Language-specific groups on authentic language usage.
- Community on research participation and contributor development.
- Core Maintainers on shared technical standards and release coordination.

## 10. Reporting

The working group shall maintain visible research objectives, experiment records and periodic progress summaries.

Material research findings, limitations, dependencies and resource requirements shall be communicated to the relevant governance authorities.
