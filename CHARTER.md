# ClevaVox — Founding Charter

**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Proposed  
**Founded and supported by:** Clevaway

## 1. Identity

ClevaVox is a community-driven open-source initiative dedicated to advancing artificial intelligence for underrepresented languages, dialects and language varieties.

ClevaVox brings together developers, researchers, linguists, native speakers, institutions and volunteers to collaboratively build accessible language technologies.

## 2. Vision

A world where every language, regardless of its geographical reach, economic significance or number of speakers, is represented and accessible through modern technology, without barriers imposed by connectivity, computing resources or financial limitations.

## 3. Mission

To collaboratively develop, maintain and openly distribute efficient AI models, datasets and tools that enable underrepresented languages to participate fully in the digital world.

ClevaVox prioritizes lightweight, edge-first and offline-capable technologies that can operate on affordable and widely available devices.

## 4. Founding pillars

### 4.1. Linguistic inclusion

Every linguistic community deserves meaningful representation in modern technology.

ClevaVox shall respect linguistic diversity, regional variations, cultural context and the participation of language communities.

### 4.2. Open source

ClevaVox shall promote open collaboration, accessible research, reproducible development and freely reusable technologies.

Software, models and datasets shall be released under appropriate open licenses wherever legally and ethically possible.

Any restrictions shall be clearly documented.

### 4.3. Edge-first artificial intelligence

ClevaVox shall prioritize efficient models that minimize computational, memory, storage and connectivity requirements.

Where technically feasible, core model capabilities should operate locally without requiring continuous access to cloud infrastructure.

Model efficiency shall be treated as a fundamental measure of success alongside linguistic accuracy and quality.

## 5. Scope

ClevaVox may develop and support:

- Speech recognition and transcription.
- Text-to-speech and speech synthesis.
- Machine translation.
- Language understanding and generation.
- Language identification and processing tools.
- Linguistic datasets and benchmarks.
- Model compression and optimization.
- Edge-device inference systems.
- Developer libraries, SDKs and reference applications.
- Research and educational resources.

The initiative is not restricted to any specific language, region, hardware platform or model architecture.

## 6. Community participation

Participation shall be open to individuals and organisations that support the initiative's mission and comply with its policies.

Contributions may include software development, AI research, language expertise, data collection, annotation, validation, testing, documentation, outreach and material or financial support.

All meaningful forms of contribution shall be recognized.

## 7. Governance

ClevaVox shall operate under a publicly documented governance framework.

The initiative shall initially be stewarded by its founders and progressively delegate responsibilities to qualified community members.

Leadership appointments shall reflect competence, commitment, integrity and accountability.

Language communities shall have meaningful representation in decisions concerning their languages and linguistic resources.

## 8. Responsible data and AI

ClevaVox shall respect contributor consent, privacy, intellectual property and the cultural interests of language communities.

Datasets and models shall undergo appropriate technical, linguistic, ethical and licensing reviews before publication.

Known limitations and material risks shall be openly documented.

## 9. Financial sustainability

ClevaVox may receive donations, grants, sponsorships, infrastructure credits and institutional support.

Resources shall be administered transparently and used consistently with the initiative's mission and applicable funding obligations.

Financial support shall not automatically grant ownership or governance authority.

## 10. Relationship with Clevaway

Clevaway serves as the founding and supporting organisation of ClevaVox.

The stewardship relationship shall be governed by the publicly documented Clevaway Stewardship Policy.

## 11. Long-term continuity

ClevaVox shall establish structures, policies and processes that enable the initiative to continue beyond the involvement of its original founders.

## 12. Amendments

This charter may be amended through the established governance process, including appropriate community consultation.

Amendments shall be publicly documented and version-controlled.
